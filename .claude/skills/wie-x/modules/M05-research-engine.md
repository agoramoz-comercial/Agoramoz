## MODULE 05 — RESEARCH ENGINE


### 5.1 Research Before Decisions

Research when:

- Current information is required.
- Technologies may have changed.
- Compatibility matters.
- External APIs are involved.
- Security is relevant.
- The project requires reference analysis.
- The user explicitly requests investigation.
- Evidence is needed to make consequential decisions.

Do not research unnecessarily for trivial tasks.

### 5.2 Source Hierarchy

Tier 1:

- Official documentation.
- Official repositories.
- Standards.
- Vendor documentation.
- Primary sources.

Tier 2:

- Source code.
- Release notes.
- Issues.
- Pull requests.
- Technical benchmarks.
- Primary technical evidence.

Tier 3:

- Expert analysis.
- Technical articles.
- Case studies.
- Credible independent reviews.

Tier 4:

- Community discussions.
- Reddit.
- Forums.
- Developer experience.

Community opinions are useful signals, not definitive proof.

### 5.3 Evidence Labels

Classify findings:

- VERIFIED — directly confirmed.
- OBSERVED — directly seen, but not independently confirmed.
- INFERRED — logically derived.
- PROPOSED — recommendation.
- ASSUMED — adopted to continue.
- UNKNOWN — not established.

### 5.4 Research Stop Condition

Stop research when:

- Requirements are sufficiently understood.
- Relevant technologies have been evaluated.
- Critical decisions have evidence.
- Uncertainty is documented.
- Additional research is unlikely to materially improve the outcome.

Do not perform endless research.

### 5.5 Research Deliverable

For substantial projects:

- Research question.
- Findings.
- Sources.
- Evidence.
- Relevant technologies.
- Alternatives.
- Compatibility.
- Licensing considerations.
- Security considerations.
- Recommendation.
- Uncertainties.
- Decision impact.
