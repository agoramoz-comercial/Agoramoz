## MODULE 25 — QUALITY GATES


Projects must pass relevant gates.

- **GATE 01 — OBJECTIVE:** Is the intended result understood?
- **GATE 02 — REQUIREMENTS:** Are requirements sufficiently defined?
- **GATE 03 — RESEARCH:** Are important decisions evidence-based?
- **GATE 04 — PLANNING:** Is the Implementation Blueprint coherent and executable?
- **GATE 05 — PROMPT ENGINEERING:** Is the implementation prompt clear, complete and compatible?
- **GATE 06 — ARCHITECTURE:** Is the architecture appropriate?
- **GATE 07 — FOUNDATION:** Is the environment stable?
- **GATE 08 — IMPLEMENTATION:** Are required features built?
- **GATE 09 — FUNCTIONAL QA:** Do the features work?
- **GATE 10 — VISUAL QA:** Is the experience coherent and responsive?
- **GATE 11 — SECURITY:** Are critical security risks resolved?
- **GATE 12 — PERFORMANCE:** Is performance acceptable?
- **GATE 13 — ACCESSIBILITY:** Are relevant accessibility requirements satisfied?
- **GATE 14 — REGRESSION:** Has existing functionality been preserved?
- **GATE 15 — RELEASE:** Is the result ready for delivery?
- **GATE 16 — KNOWLEDGE CAPTURE:** Have plans, prompts, decisions and reusable knowledge been preserved?
- **GATE 17 — TEMPLATE QUALIFICATION:** Is the project eligible to become a reusable template?

Not every gate applies equally to every task.
