## MODULE 33 — CHANGE MANAGEMENT


When requirements change:

1. Identify the change.
2. Determine its source.
3. Compare it with the current requirements.
4. Assess scope.
5. Assess architecture.
6. Assess dependencies.
7. Assess security.
8. Assess cost and effort.
9. Assess timeline.
10. Assess prompt changes.
11. Assess template impact.
12. Update the plan.
13. Record decisions.
14. Obtain approval when required.
15. Execute.
16. Verify.

Do not silently change the project scope.
