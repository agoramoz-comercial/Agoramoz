## MODULE 38 — UNIVERSAL ENGINEERING WORKFLOW


For complex projects, use the following integrated workflow:

- **STEP 01 — INTAKE:** Understand the request and objective.
- **STEP 02 — CLASSIFICATION:** Determine task type, complexity and risk.
- **STEP 03 — CAPABILITY DETECTION:** Identify available tools and platform constraints.
- **STEP 04 — REQUIREMENTS:** Extract and normalize requirements.
- **STEP 05 — TEMPLATE SEARCH:** Search for reusable, relevant, qualified templates.
- **STEP 06 — REUSE ASSESSMENT:** Determine reuse, adaptation, replacement, revalidation and rejection.
- **STEP 07 — RESEARCH:** Investigate relevant evidence and technologies.
- **STEP 08 — PRODUCT DESIGN:** Define user needs, scope, features and acceptance criteria.
- **STEP 09 — IMPLEMENTATION PLANNING:** The IPE creates the execution blueprint.
- **STEP 10 — ARCHITECTURE:** Design the system.
- **STEP 11 — PROMPT ENGINEERING:** The APETA compiles the Master Implementation Prompt and specialist instructions.
- **STEP 12 — PROMPT RED TEAM:** Identify and correct prompt weaknesses.
- **STEP 13 — PROMPT TESTING:** Test representative scenarios.
- **STEP 14 — APPROVAL GATE:** Obtain approval when required.
- **STEP 15 — FOUNDATION:** Establish protected environment and baseline.
- **STEP 16 — IMPLEMENTATION:** Execute tasks according to the blueprint.
- **STEP 17 — INTEGRATION:** Integrate features, components, systems and data.
- **STEP 18 — QA:** Run relevant functional, visual, security, performance and accessibility checks.
- **STEP 19 — RED TEAM:** Challenge the final result.
- **STEP 20 — CORRECTION:** Resolve failures and retest.
- **STEP 21 — FINAL VERIFICATION:** Confirm actual completion against requirements.
- **STEP 22 — DOCUMENTATION:** Preserve the project state, decisions, results and limitations.
- **STEP 23 — RETROSPECTIVE:** Evaluate what worked and failed.
- **STEP 24 — TEMPLATE QUALIFICATION:** Assess whether the project can become a reusable template.
- **STEP 25 — TEMPLATE PROMOTION:** Promote only when qualification requirements are satisfied and approval is obtained where required.
- **STEP 26 — DELIVERY:** Provide the final result, evidence, limitations and next action.
