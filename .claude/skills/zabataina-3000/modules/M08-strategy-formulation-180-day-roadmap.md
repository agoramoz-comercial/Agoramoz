## SECTION 001 — STRATEGY MANDATE

A strategy is a set of coherent choices about where to compete, how to win and what not to do, backed by the resources to execute.

A list of activities is not a strategy.

Prerequisites (Module 01, Section 012): an approved Executive Intelligence Brief, a feasibility verdict and an identified constraint. Without them, this module must not produce a roadmap.

---

## SECTION 002 — STRATEGIC CHOICES

Every strategy must answer:

1. **Objective:** Which financial track(s) and target(s), by when?
2. **Where to play:** Which segments, geographies, offers and channels?
3. **How to win:** What advantage makes customers choose us?
4. **Constraint focus:** Which constraint must be relieved first?
5. **Priority initiatives:** Which three to five initiatives (Z-PRIORITY ≥ 3.0)?
6. **What we will not do:** Explicit exclusions.
7. **Resources:** Capital, people and time allocated.
8. **Risks:** Main risks and mitigations.
9. **Measures:** KPIs and gate criteria.

### 002.1 — Strategy statement

```
STRATEGY STATEMENT
In the next 180 days, [organization] will reach [target] by
selling [offer] to [segment] through [channels],
winning because [advantage],
focusing first on relieving [constraint],
and will not [exclusions].
```

### 002.2 — Strategic options

Present at least two credible strategic options plus the recommended option, in the Executive Decision Standard (Module 01, Section 008). Include the "minimum-risk" option.

## SECTION 003 — GROWTH STRATEGY LIBRARY

| Strategy | When it fits |
|---|---|
| Monetize existing customers (upsell, cross-sell, reactivation) | Existing customer base with unmet needs |
| Price and offer optimization | Underpriced or undifferentiated offer |
| Conversion optimization | Leads exist, conversion is low |
| Channel expansion | Proven offer, limited reach |
| Segment focus / niche domination | Broad, diluted positioning |
| Large-account / institutional sales | High-value buyers accessible |
| Partnerships and distribution | Partners own access to customers |
| Productization of services | Repeatable service delivered manually |
| Recurring revenue conversion | Repeat buyers on one-off transactions |
| Geographic expansion | Proven model, saturated home market |
| Acquisition / consolidation | Capital available, fragmented market |

Prefer strategies closest to existing customers, assets and capabilities for the 180-day horizon. Distance from the core increases risk and time to cash.

## SECTION 004 — THE 180-DAY GATED ROADMAP

The 180 days are divided into four phases separated by decision gates.

| Phase | Days | Focus | Exit gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 — Foundation & Validation | 1–30 | Fix critical gaps, validate offer and channel with real sales | G1 |
| P2 — Traction | 31–75 | Build a repeatable acquisition and sales engine | G2 |
| P3 — Acceleration | 76–135 | Scale what works; optimize unit economics | G3 |
| P4 — Consolidation & Scale | 136–180 | Systematize, protect margins, prepare next horizon | G4 |

### 004.1 — Gate criteria

Each gate requires a formal review:

```
GATE REVIEW — G[n] — [Date]
Planned results vs actual results (by KPI)
Track A–D progress
Unit economics status
Cash position vs plan
Constraint status
Experiments: continued / stopped / scaled
Risks: new / changed
Feasibility re-assessment of targets
DECISION: Proceed / Adjust / Pivot / Stop
Approved by:
```

Default gate thresholds (adjusted per engagement):

| Gate | Default pass criteria |
|---|---|
| G1 | Offer sold to real customers at target price; at least one channel with measurable conversion; cash plan secured |
| G2 | Repeatable lead flow; positive contribution margin; CAC known; pipeline covering next phase |
| G3 | Revenue run-rate on trajectory for the target (or target formally revised); unit economics within limits |
| G4 | Processes documented; KPI system operating; next 180-day plan drafted |

### 004.2 — Gate discipline

1. Do not proceed to scale before passing G2.
2. If a gate is failed, apply Module 02, Section 013.3 (failure analysis) and choose Adjust, Pivot or Stop.
3. Revise targets openly when evidence demands it (Module 01, Section 014).

## SECTION 006 — EXECUTION PLANNING

### 006.1 — Work breakdown

Break each initiative into weekly milestones with owners. No task without an owner; no milestone without a date.

### 006.2 — Operating rhythm

| Rhythm | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Daily (optional) | Blockers and priorities for execution teams |
| Weekly review | KPIs, actions, blockers, cash |
| Monthly review | Financial results, initiative performance, re-prioritization |
| Gate review | Formal go/no-go decision |

### 006.3 — First 14 days

The first two weeks must produce early, visible commercial results where possible: reactivating past customers, closing open pipeline, fixing obvious pricing or conversion leaks, collecting receivables. Early cash funds later phases and builds confidence.
