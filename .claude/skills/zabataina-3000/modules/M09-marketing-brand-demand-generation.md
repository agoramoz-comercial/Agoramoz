## SECTION 001 — MARKETING MANDATE

Marketing exists to produce qualified demand and revenue, not impressions.

Every marketing activity must be connected to a funnel stage, a segment (Module 05), an offer (Module 07) and a measurable outcome (leads, opportunities, sales, CAC).

Vanity metrics (followers, likes, impressions, traffic) are supporting indicators only (Module 01, Principle 4).


### 002.2 — Message hierarchy

1. **Headline promise:** outcome for the segment.
2. **Problem agitation:** the cost of the current situation, in customer language (Module 05).
3. **Mechanism:** why our approach works.
4. **Proof:** real results, cases, data, credentials.
5. **Offer and risk reversal.**
6. **Call to action.**

Messages must use the exact language captured in customer research. Claims must be substantiated.

### 002.3 — Brand essentials

For the 180-day horizon, brand work is limited to what improves trust and conversion: clear name and identity, consistent visual system, credible proof assets, professional presence on the channels customers use. Large brand investments require a demonstrated commercial case.

## SECTION 003 — CHANNEL STRATEGY

### 003.1 — Channel catalogue

| Channel | Speed to results | Cost profile | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Direct outreach (email, phone, LinkedIn, WhatsApp) | Fast | Time-intensive | B2B, high-value offers |
| Existing customers and referrals | Fast | Low | All businesses with a customer base |
| Partnerships and affiliates | Medium | Revenue share | Access to established audiences |
| Paid search | Fast | Variable, auction-based | Existing demand with search intent |
| Paid social | Fast | Variable | Visual/consumer offers, demand creation |
| Content and SEO | Slow | Time-intensive, compounding | Long-term authority and inbound |
| Events, trade shows, communities | Medium | Moderate to high | B2B relationships, local markets |
| Public relations and media | Variable | Low to moderate | Credibility, launches |
| Marketplaces and platforms | Fast | Commission | Products with platform demand |
| Field / physical sales and retail | Medium | High | Local and physical products |

### 003.2 — Channel selection rules

1. Go where the ICP already spends attention (Module 05, ICP).
2. For the 180-day mission, prioritize channels with fast feedback and fast cash: existing customers, referrals, direct outreach, partnerships and intent-based paid search.
3. Test a maximum of two to three new channels at once.
4. Scale a channel only when its CAC payback fits within runway and contribution margin is positive (Module 06, Section 003.3).
5. Adapt to geography: messaging apps, local platforms, payment methods and media differ by market (Module 01, Section 004).

### 003.3 — Channel experiment card

Use the experiment card of Module 02, Section 014.3, with channel-specific fields: audience, creative/message, budget, cost per lead target, cost per opportunity target, CAC target.

## SECTION 004 — FUNNEL ARCHITECTURE

```
AWARENESS → INTEREST → LEAD → QUALIFIED LEAD (MQL) → SALES OPPORTUNITY (SQL) → CUSTOMER
```

For each stage define: the asset, the conversion mechanism, the KPI and the target conversion rate.

| Stage | Typical assets | KPI |
|---|---|---|
| Awareness | Ads, content, PR, outreach | Reach, CTR, reply rate |
| Interest | Landing pages, videos, case studies | Engagement, time, clicks |
| Lead | Lead magnet, diagnostic, consultation offer | Cost per lead, conversion rate |
| Qualified | Qualification form, scoring, nurture sequence | Lead-to-MQL rate |
| Opportunity | Booked call, demo, proposal request | MQL-to-SQL rate, cost per opportunity |

Handover rules between marketing and sales are defined in Module 10.

## SECTION 006 — MARKETING METRICS

| Metric | Formula |
|---|---|
| Cost per lead (CPL) | Spend ÷ Leads |
| Cost per opportunity | Spend ÷ Sales opportunities |
| Marketing CAC | Marketing spend ÷ New customers from marketing |
| ROAS | Revenue attributed ÷ Ad spend |
| Contribution ROAS | (Revenue × Contribution margin %) ÷ Ad spend |
| Lead-to-customer rate | Customers ÷ Leads |

Use contribution ROAS, not revenue ROAS, to judge profitability. A revenue ROAS above 1 can still lose money.

Attribution must be stated (first touch, last touch, multi-touch, self-reported). Acknowledge attribution uncertainty.

## SECTION 007 — COMPLIANCE

Marketing must comply with data-protection law (consent for communications, lawful basis for data processing), advertising standards, platform policies, consumer protection and anti-spam rules in each jurisdiction. Never purchase or use personal data without a lawful basis. Escalate uncertain cases for legal review.
