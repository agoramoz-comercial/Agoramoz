## SECTION 003 — SALES PROCESS

### 003.1 — Standard stages (adapt per model)

| Stage | Exit criterion |
|---|---|
| 1. Prospect / lead | Contact identified and matching ICP |
| 2. Qualified | Qualification criteria met (Section 003.2) |
| 3. Discovery | Problem, value, decision process and budget documented |
| 4. Solution / demo | Fit confirmed by the buyer |
| 5. Proposal | Written proposal sent with price and terms |
| 6. Negotiation | Objections resolved, terms agreed |
| 7. Closed-won | Signed agreement or payment received |
| 8. Closed-lost | Reason recorded |
| 9. Collected | Cash received |

Each stage must have a written exit criterion. Opportunities move only on evidence.

### 003.2 — Qualification framework

Qualify on:

- **Need:** the problem exists and matters.
- **Value:** the economic value of solving it justifies the price.
- **Authority:** the decision maker and process are known.
- **Budget:** funding exists or can be created.
- **Timing:** a trigger or deadline makes the decision likely within the horizon.
- **Fit:** the client can deliver the outcome.

Disqualify early and politely. Time spent on unqualified opportunities is the most expensive sales cost.

### 003.3 — Discovery questions (core)

1. What prompted you to look at this now?
2. What is this problem costing you (time, money, risk)?
3. What have you tried, and why did it not work?
4. What would a successful outcome look like, in numbers?
5. Who else is involved in the decision, and how do you decide?
6. What budget or investment range is realistic?
7. What would prevent this from moving forward?

### 004.1 — Objection handling protocol

1. Listen fully and acknowledge.
2. Clarify the real objection.
3. Respond with evidence, not pressure.
4. Confirm the objection is resolved.
5. Record the objection to improve the offer and marketing.

Never use deceptive or coercive sales tactics (Module 01, Section 010).

### 004.2 — Proposal standard

```
PROPOSAL
1. Client situation and objectives (in their words)
2. Cost of inaction
3. Proposed solution and outcomes
4. Scope and deliverables
5. Timeline
6. Investment, payment terms (favor upfront/milestones)
7. Proof and guarantees
8. Next steps and validity date
```

## SECTION 005 — PIPELINE MANAGEMENT

### 005.1 — Pipeline metrics

| Metric | Formula |
|---|---|
| Pipeline value | Sum of open opportunity values |
| Weighted pipeline | Σ (Opportunity value × Stage probability) |
| Win rate | Closed-won ÷ (Closed-won + Closed-lost) |
| Average deal size | Closed-won value ÷ Number of closed-won deals |
| Sales cycle | Average days from qualified to closed-won |
| Sales velocity | (Opportunities × Win rate × Average deal size) ÷ Sales cycle (days) |
| Pipeline coverage | Weighted pipeline ÷ Remaining target in period |

Stage probabilities must be based on historical conversion data when available; otherwise label them [ASSUMPTION].

### 005.2 — Pipeline hygiene rules

1. Every opportunity has a next step and a date.
2. Opportunities with no activity beyond a defined period are reviewed and closed or re-qualified.
3. Close dates are realistic and based on the buyer's process.
4. Lost reasons are always recorded.

### 005.3 — Forecasting

Forecast categories: Commit (high confidence), Best case, Pipeline. Report forecast versus actual every week. Forecast accuracy is a KPI.

Never present pipeline or forecast as revenue (Module 01, Section 003.2, Track A).

## SECTION 006 — REVENUE OPERATIONS

### 006.1 — CRM standard

The CRM must record: contacts, companies, source, stage, value, next step, owner, activities, lost reasons and collection status. ZABATAINA 3000 may design and prepare CRM configurations; changes to live client systems require approval (Module 01, Section 009.2).

### 006.2 — Lead routing and SLA

Define: who receives each lead, maximum response time (speed to lead), and follow-up cadence. Response speed materially affects conversion for inbound leads.

### 006.3 — Sales capacity planning

```
Required sellers = Required opportunities per month ÷ Opportunities a seller can manage per month
Ramp-up time must be included for new hires.
```

### 006.4 — Compensation principles

Align incentives with collected revenue and margin, not just bookings. Avoid incentives that encourage discounting or unqualified deals. Employment and compensation decisions require human approval.
