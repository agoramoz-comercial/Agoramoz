## SECTION 001 — MANDATE

Acquiring a customer is an investment; retention and expansion are the return on that investment.

Retention multiplies LTV, improves LTV:CAC, stabilizes cash and increases enterprise value (Tracks C and D).

---

## SECTION 002 — RETENTION METRICS

| Metric | Formula |
|---|---|
| Customer churn rate | Customers lost in period ÷ Customers at start of period |
| Revenue churn (gross) | Recurring revenue lost (cancellations + downgrades) ÷ Recurring revenue at start |
| Gross revenue retention (GRR) | (Start recurring revenue − churn − contraction) ÷ Start recurring revenue |
| Net revenue retention (NRR) | (Start recurring revenue − churn − contraction + expansion) ÷ Start recurring revenue |
| Repeat purchase rate | Customers with 2+ purchases ÷ Total customers (transactional) |
| Time to first value | Days from purchase to first measurable outcome |
| Net Promoter Score (NPS) | % promoters − % detractors |

NRR above 100% means the existing base grows without new customers.

## SECTION 003 — CUSTOMER SUCCESS SYSTEM

### 003.1 — Onboarding

1. Welcome and expectation setting within 24 hours of purchase.
2. Defined success milestones agreed with the customer.
3. Fastest possible path to first value.
4. Checkpoint at the first milestone.

### 003.2 — Customer health score

Combine indicators such as usage or engagement, outcome achievement, support issues, payment behavior and relationship strength. Classify customers as Healthy / At risk / Critical and define actions for each.

### 003.3 — Churn prevention

1. Identify leading indicators of churn from historical data.
2. Trigger intervention when indicators appear.
3. Conduct exit interviews; record reasons.
4. Address systemic churn causes in offer, delivery or targeting.

### 003.4 — Win-back

Design reactivation campaigns for lapsed customers with a relevant offer. Past customers are often the cheapest source of revenue in the first 30 days of an engagement.

## SECTION 004 — EXPANSION

| Lever | Description |
|---|---|
| Upsell | Higher tier or larger scope |
| Cross-sell | Complementary offer |
| Usage growth | More units, users or volume |
| Recurring conversion | One-off buyers moved to subscriptions or retainers |
| Renewal with improved terms | Longer contracts, prepayment |

Expansion offers must be triggered by customer success, not pushed regardless of value.

---

## SECTION 005 — REFERRALS AND ADVOCACY

1. Ask for referrals at moments of demonstrated value.
2. Make referring easy and, where appropriate and legal, rewarded.
3. Collect real testimonials and case studies with written consent.
4. Never fabricate or incentivize false reviews (Module 01, Section 010).

---

## SECTION 006 — SERVICE QUALITY AND FEEDBACK

1. Define service standards (response time, resolution time, quality criteria).
2. Collect feedback systematically (surveys, interviews, support data).
3. Close the loop: tell customers what changed because of their feedback.
4. Feed insights into product, offer and marketing (Modules 05, 07, 09).
