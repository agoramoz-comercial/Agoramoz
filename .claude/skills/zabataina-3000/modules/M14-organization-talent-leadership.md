## SECTION 001 — MANDATE

Strategy is executed by people. An organization without clear ownership, capacity and accountability cannot deliver a 180-day transformation.

Employment decisions — hiring, firing, compensation and contractual changes — are human decisions requiring explicit approval and compliance with local labor law (Module 01, Section 009.2).

---

## SECTION 002 — ORGANIZATIONAL DIAGNOSIS

Assess:

1. Current structure and reporting lines.
2. Roles and responsibilities (documented or implicit).
3. Capacity versus workload by role.
4. Skills gaps relative to the strategy.
5. Key-person dependencies, especially on the founder.
6. Decision rights and bottlenecks in decision-making.
7. Culture: accountability, speed, learning, customer focus.

### 002.1 — Founder bottleneck test

If most sales, delivery decisions and approvals depend on the founder, the founder is likely the binding constraint. The plan must include delegation, documentation and role design to release founder time for the highest-value activities.

## SECTION 003 — STRUCTURE DESIGN

### 003.1 — Design principles

1. Structure follows strategy: organize around the priority initiatives and the revenue engine.
2. Every outcome has one accountable owner.
3. Keep layers minimal at small and medium scale.
4. Separate "run the business" from "change the business" responsibilities when capacity allows.

### 003.2 — Accountability matrix (RACI)

For each priority initiative and core process define who is Responsible, Accountable, Consulted and Informed. Only one Accountable per item.

### 003.3 — Role scorecard


## SECTION 004 — TALENT STRATEGY

### 004.1 — Build, buy, borrow, automate

| Option | Use when |
|---|---|
| Build (train existing team) | Skills are learnable within the time frame |
| Buy (hire) | Permanent, core capability needed |
| Borrow (freelancers, agencies, consultants, partners) | Speed, specialist or temporary need |
| Automate (Module 13) | Repetitive, rule-based work |

For a 180-day horizon, "borrow" and "automate" are often faster and less risky than permanent hires.

### 004.2 — Hiring process

1. Role scorecard approved.
2. Sourcing plan.
3. Structured interviews against scorecard competencies.
4. Work sample or practical test.
5. Reference checks.
6. Offer compliant with local law.
7. 30/60/90-day onboarding plan with outcomes.

### 004.3 — Hiring economics

```
Fully loaded cost = Salary + taxes + benefits + equipment + recruitment + management time
Expected value = Revenue enabled or cost saved by the role
Ramp-up time to productivity must be included in the cash plan (Module 06).
```

## SECTION 005 — PERFORMANCE AND ACCOUNTABILITY

1. Objectives cascaded from company targets to teams and individuals.
2. Weekly review of KPIs and commitments (Module 08, Section 006.2).
3. Recognition linked to results and values.
4. Early, factual feedback on underperformance with support and clear expectations.
5. Incentives aligned with collected revenue, margin and quality, not activity alone.

## SECTION 006 — LEADERSHIP AND CHANGE

### 006.1 — Leadership operating rhythm

| Meeting | Frequency | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Leadership weekly | Weekly | KPIs, cash, priorities, blockers |
| Initiative reviews | Weekly/biweekly | Progress against charters |
| Monthly business review | Monthly | Financials, strategy, people |
| Gate review | Per gate | Go/no-go decisions |

### 006.2 — Change management

1. Explain why the change is needed (with evidence).
2. Define what changes for each role.
3. Involve those affected in designing the how.
4. Provide training and tools.
5. Celebrate early wins.
6. Monitor adoption and resistance; address causes.
