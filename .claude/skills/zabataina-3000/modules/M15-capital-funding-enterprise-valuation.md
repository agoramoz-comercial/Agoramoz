## SECTION 001 — MANDATE

Capital is a tool to accelerate a working business model, not a substitute for one.

All funding and valuation work is analytical and preparatory. Regulated investment advice, securities offerings, legal structuring and tax planning require qualified professionals (Module 01, Section 010). Signing agreements and accepting funds require explicit approval.

---

## SECTION 002 — CAPITAL REQUIREMENT

```
Capital required = Peak cumulative negative cash flow in the conservative scenario (Module 06)
                 + Minimum safety balance
                 + Contingency (default 15–25%)
```

Before raising external capital, exhaust internal sources: price increases, upfront payments, collections, cost reduction and asset sales (Module 06, Section 005.2).

## SECTION 003 — FUNDING OPTIONS

| Source | Cost / dilution | Speed | Suitable when |
|---|---|---|---|
| Customer funding (prepayments, deposits) | No dilution | Fast | Strong offer and trust |
| Retained earnings / bootstrapping | No dilution | Ongoing | Profitable operations |
| Supplier credit | Low | Fast | Good supplier relationships |
| Bank loans / credit lines | Interest, collateral | Medium | Stable cash flows, assets |
| Invoice / receivables financing | Fee | Fast | B2B with creditworthy customers |
| Revenue-based financing | Revenue share | Medium | Recurring, predictable revenue |
| Grants and development programs | Usually non-dilutive | Slow | Eligible sectors and geographies |
| Development finance / impact investors | Variable | Slow | Emerging markets, social impact |
| Angel investors | Equity | Medium | Early stage, high growth potential |
| Venture capital | Significant equity | Slow | Large market, scalable model |
| Strategic partners / corporate investors | Equity or contracts | Medium | Strategic synergies |
| Crowdfunding | Variable | Medium | Consumer appeal, community |

### 003.1 — Selection criteria

Evaluate cost of capital, dilution, control, covenants, speed, repayment risk under the conservative scenario and fit with the business stage.

Never recommend debt that the conservative scenario cannot service.

1. Company overview and legal documents.
2. Financial statements and management accounts.
3. Financial model with scenarios (Module 06).
4. Unit economics and KPI history.
5. Customer list and contracts (with confidentiality controls).
6. Cap table.

### 004.2 — Pitch narrative

Problem → Solution → Market → Traction (verified) → Business model and unit economics → Competition → Team → Financial plan → Funding ask and use of funds.

All traction and financial figures must be verified and labeled. Never overstate traction to investors.

## SECTION 005 — ENTERPRISE VALUATION (TRACK D)

### 005.1 — Methods

| Method | Basis | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Discounted cash flow (DCF) | Present value of future free cash flows | Predictable cash flows |
| Comparable company multiples | Multiples of revenue, EBITDA or earnings of similar companies | Companies with comparables |
| Precedent transactions | Multiples paid in comparable acquisitions | M&A context |
| Asset-based | Net value of assets | Asset-heavy or distressed |
| Earnings multiple (SDE/EBITDA) | Multiple of owner earnings | Small private businesses |
| Venture method | Expected exit value discounted at target return | Early-stage startups |

Use at least two methods and present a range, not a single number.

### 005.2 — Valuation formulas (reference)

```
EV (multiple) = Metric (e.g., EBITDA) × Applicable multiple
EV (DCF) = Σ FCF_t ÷ (1 + r)^t + Terminal value ÷ (1 + r)^n
Equity value = Enterprise value − Net debt
```

Multiples must come from cited, relevant, recent sources adjusted for size, geography, growth and risk. Private small businesses typically trade at lower multiples than public companies; emerging-market risk must be reflected.

### 005.3 — Value drivers

| Driver | Effect on value |
|---|---|
| Revenue growth | Higher |
| Profitability and cash conversion | Higher |
| Recurring revenue and NRR | Higher |
| Customer diversification | Higher (concentration lowers value) |
| Documented processes, low key-person dependency | Higher |
| Defensible IP, brand, contracts | Higher |
| Clean financial records and governance | Higher |
| Legal, tax or compliance issues | Lower |

The 180-day plan should improve the drivers, not only the headline metric.

### 005.4 — Valuation integrity rules

1. A valuation estimate is not cash, an offer or a sale price (Module 01, Track D).
2. State methods, assumptions, sources and range.
3. Recommend independent professional valuation for transactions, tax, legal or investor purposes.

## SECTION 008 — MODULE DIRECTIVE

Fund what works; fix what does not.

Use the cheapest capital first: customers and cash flow.

Never borrow beyond what the conservative scenario can repay.

Value is built by profits, growth, recurrence and lower risk.

**CAPITAL ACCELERATES; IT DOES NOT CREATE.**
