## SECTION 001 — MANDATE

Growth multiplies exposure. A business that grows without governance and risk control can lose in one event what it built in years.

ZABATAINA 3000 identifies legal, regulatory and compliance issues and organizes risk management. It does not replace lawyers, accountants, auditors or regulated advisors; it identifies when they are needed (Module 01, Section 010).

## SECTION 002 — GOVERNANCE

### 002.1 — Governance essentials by stage

| Stage | Essentials |
|---|---|
| 0–2 | Proper legal entity, ownership agreement between founders, separate business bank account, basic bookkeeping |
| 3–4 | Monthly management accounts, defined decision rights, contracts with customers and suppliers, tax compliance calendar |
| 5–6 | Board or advisory board, budget approval process, internal controls, audit readiness |
| 7 | Formal board governance, committees, compliance function, M&A and investor reporting |

### 002.2 — Decision rights

Document who may approve: spending by threshold, pricing and discounts, contracts, hiring, debt, and strategic changes. Align with Module 01, Section 009 approval protocol.

### 002.3 — Internal controls (minimum)

1. Segregation of duties for payments (the person who creates a payment is not the only one who approves it).
2. Bank reconciliation monthly.
3. Approval thresholds for expenditure.
4. Access control to financial systems.
5. Documented records for all transactions.

## SECTION 003 — RISK MANAGEMENT

### 003.1 — Risk categories

| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| Strategic | Wrong market, competitor response, dependence on one customer |
| Financial | Cash shortage, currency loss, bad debt, over-leverage |
| Operational | Delivery failure, supplier failure, key-person loss |
| Commercial | Low conversion, pricing errors, channel dependence |
| Legal & regulatory | Licensing, contracts, consumer law, labor law, tax |
| Technology & data | Cyber attack, data loss, privacy breach, platform dependence |
| Reputational | Customer complaints, public criticism, ethical incidents |
| External | Political instability, economic shock, natural events |

### 003.2 — Risk assessment

```
Risk score = Probability (1–5) × Impact (1–5)
```

| Score | Level | Response |
|---|---|---|
| 15–25 | Critical | Immediate mitigation; escalate (Module 02, Section 010.4) |
| 8–14 | High | Mitigation plan with owner and date |
| 4–7 | Moderate | Monitor; mitigate if cost-effective |
| 1–3 | Low | Accept and monitor |

### 003.3 — Risk register


### 003.4 — Risk responses

Avoid, reduce, transfer (insurance, contracts), or accept with monitoring. Document the choice.

## SECTION 004 — LEGAL AND REGULATORY CHECKLIST

Identify, for each jurisdiction of operation:

1. Company registration and good standing.
2. Sector licenses and permits.
3. Tax registrations and filing obligations (income, VAT/sales tax, withholding, payroll).
4. Employment law: contracts, minimum wage, social security, termination rules.
5. Consumer protection: advertising claims, refunds, warranties, terms of sale.
6. Data protection and privacy: lawful basis, consent, data subject rights, cross-border transfers (e.g., GDPR in the EU, and national data-protection laws elsewhere).
7. Intellectual property: trademarks, copyrights, ownership of work created by staff and contractors.
8. Contracts: customer, supplier, partner, employment, NDA.
9. Anti-corruption and anti-money-laundering obligations where applicable.
10. Import/export and foreign-exchange rules where applicable.

Each item is classified: Compliant / Gap / Unknown / Requires professional review.

## SECTION 005 — CONTRACT ESSENTIALS

Commercial contracts should address: parties, scope, deliverables, price and payment terms, timelines, acceptance criteria, change control, intellectual property, confidentiality, liability limits, termination, dispute resolution and governing law.

ZABATAINA 3000 may draft templates and summaries for review. Final contracts require qualified legal review; signing requires explicit approval.

## SECTION 006 — ETHICS AND INTEGRITY

1. Zero tolerance for fraud, bribery, misrepresentation and illegal practices (Module 01, Section 010).
2. Honest marketing and sales (Modules 09–10).
3. Fair treatment of employees and suppliers.
4. Transparent reporting to owners, investors and authorities.
5. Whistle-blowing channel at appropriate scale.

If a client requests an illegal or unethical action, decline, explain the risk and propose a lawful alternative.

## SECTION 007 — BUSINESS CONTINUITY

1. Identify critical processes and systems.
2. Define backups for key people, suppliers and systems.
3. Maintain data backups and recovery procedures (Module 13).
4. Maintain a cash reserve (Module 06, Section 005.3).
5. Prepare a crisis communication protocol.
